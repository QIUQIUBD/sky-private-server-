SkyNet 实时联机协议 (逆向推断)
================================
连接流程:
  1. 客户端向 AccountServer (skygold.top) 发起 /account/login 等请求
  2. AccountServer 返回 GameServer 信息(hostIp 等)
  3. SkyNetClient 按 IP 直连 GameServer: "Attempting to connect to SkyNet at <ip>"
  4. 建立 WebSocket (SocketRocket/SRWebSocket) 长连接
  5. 序列化: protobuf-c (protobuf 2.0.3)

证据字符串:
  - "Attempting to connect to SkyNet at "
  - "Established connection to SkyNet at "
  - "Closing old connection, incorrect IP"  (按IP重连)
  - hostIp
  - ". Disconnecting from SkyNet temporarily." (断线)
  - SkyNetClient 方法签名:
      void init(Game*, HttpClient*)            -- 初始+HTTP
      void bindLua(lua_State*, NetModule*, NetReachability*)
      void sendCommand(const char*)            -- 调试命令
      void update()                            -- 每帧
  - TLS: Apple Security框架(SSLHandshake/SSLCopyPeerTrust) + CC_SHA256
  - SRWebSocket allowsUntrustedSSLCertificates = 允许非信任证书(测试服)
  - Lua侧: game:skyNetClient():SendCommandResponse(msg) -- 命令响应

已知RPC(NetRpc, protobuf消息):
  OfferCandleRpc/FoundYouRpc/BondSyncRpc/FriendSyncRpc/GateSyncRpc/
  OpenGateRpc/DepositRpc/UseItemRpc/WaxSyncRpc/KillPlayerRpc/
  DeathMarchRpc/ReplaySaveRpc/ReplayStopRpc/LevelUpFxRpc/...

protobuf消息类:
  AccountAuthRequest/AccountServerRequest/FriendRequest/GiftMessage/
  SendChatMessage/HandleGameMessage/CritterV2Message
